Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Lawton HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, proper tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. When a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, sucks hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and leaves staff miserable during the rush. Our technicians balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Fast Response

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Each hour without climate control costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it sends shoppers home on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Lawton HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Lawton and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on rooftop and split systems. The priority is diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible. After that, we set up maintenance and monitoring so it doesn't happen again, and each response comes with full documentation for your records.
